01

Getting started

Install the plugin, log in with Roblox, and learn what every part of the window does.

Install and open the window

Get CheckpointLTC from its Creator Store page. Once it's installed, a CheckpointLTC button shows up in the LTCLevTec section of the Plugins tab.

  • Open and close with the toolbar button, the ✕ in the title bar, or a shortcut you bind yourself.
  • Move it by dragging the title bar. Resize it from the grip in the bottom right corner. The smallest size is 360 by 440 pixels.
  • It remembers where you left it, so it opens in the same spot next time.
  • A red dot on the toolbar icon means something needs you: an unread notification, a task due soon, or a new version. It stays, window open or closed, until you deal with it on the Notifications page. If Changes to my lists is on, teammates editing your lists light it too while the window is closed.

Log in with Roblox

Plugins can't open a browser, so logging in happens on Roblox's own website. You never type your password into CheckpointLTC.

  1. Press Log in with Roblox. The window shows a short code and the button changes to Show the login link.
  2. Press Show the login link and copy the link into your browser. It works for 10 minutes.
  3. Check the code on the page matches the one in Studio, then press Continue with Roblox. That's Roblox's own permission screen.
  4. Wait a few seconds. The plugin checks every 3 seconds and finishes on its own. Press Cancel any time to stop.

What Roblox shares

  • Your UserId. That's how lists know who owns them and who they're shared with. No username, email or password is stored.
  • Whether you own the plugin, when it's a paid version. Roblox answers yes or no at login and about once a day after.
  • The account must match. Log in with the same Roblox account you're signed in to Studio with.

Other screens you might see

Sign in to Roblox Studio first.Studio isn't signed in to a Roblox account. Sign in at the top right of Studio, then try again.
This Roblox account doesn't own CheckpointLTC yetPaid versions only. Get it on the Creator Store with the same account, then log in again.
Your sign in expiredYour key was signed out from another device or replaced. Log in with Roblox again.
Your key stays on your computer. After login the plugin saves a private key in Studio's plugin settings, never inside your place. The server only keeps a scrambled copy of it.

How syncing works

Lists live on the CheckpointLTC server, not inside your place. They follow you to every place and every computer you log in on.

  • Your changes show right away. The plugin updates the screen first and saves in the background. A task that's still saving looks slightly faded.
  • If a save fails, the change rolls back and a red toast explains why.
  • Other people's changes come in on the refresh timer: every 30 seconds by default, or 10 seconds, 1 minute or 2 minutes in Settings.
  • It slows down when you do. After 3 minutes with no clicks in Studio, refreshes happen 4 times less often. With the window closed, 8 times less often. Opening the window refreshes straight away.
  • Two people, one task. If someone saved the same task text or notes before you, you get "Someone else changed this task first. I loaded their version." Nothing is silently overwritten.
  • It remembers your last list and opens it the next time you log in.

Toasts

Small messages at the bottom of the window are called toasts. Blue means info, green means done, yellow is a warning and red is an error. Some carry a button, like Undo after a delete. They close by themselves after 5 seconds, ones with a button after 12. Toasts are only for things you just did. Notifications never pop up, they wait on the Notifications page.

03

Built for Studio

Link tasks to parts and script lines, turn TODO comments into tasks, pin lists to a place, see your team, and collect bug reports from players.

Studio links

A link connects a task to a part, model, folder or script in your place. Clicking it later selects that thing, and for scripts opens the editor at the right line.

Three ways to make one

  1. From the drawer. Select something in the Explorer, open a task, press Link my selection.
  2. From the task menu. Select something, then ••• and Link to my selection.
  3. Make a new linked task. Select something and press ＋ next to the new task box. You get a task like Work on SprintBoost, and scripts get the Scripting label.

A link made from a selected script points at line 1. Links made by the TODO scanner point at the exact line of the comment.

How the link finds its way back

When you link something, CheckpointLTC puts a small hidden marker on it: an attribute and a tag. That's why links keep working after you rename or move things.

Pin to a game and Team Create

Pin to this game

Owners and admins can pin a list to the place that's open. From then on it sits under This game at the top of the sidebar whenever anyone with access opens that place.

  • Pin or unpin from the list menu.
  • Published places only. An unsaved place or a local file has no place id, so the option doesn't show.
  • One place per list. Pinning in a new place moves the pin there.

See who's around

  • Avatars in the list header show who else has this list open. Faded ones are in your Team Create session but not on the list right now.
  • ● Name on a task means that person has the task's drawer open.
  • It updates every minute, and someone drops off two minutes after they close the list or Studio.
Good habit for teams: pin your main list to the game, give everyone edit rights, and turn on Changes to my lists in Settings. Then the toolbar icon tells you when someone moved something, even with the window closed.

Player reports from your game

Let players send bug reports and feedback from inside your live game. They land in the list's In game reports inbox, where you approve them into tasks.

  1. Open the list, then ••• and In game reports inbox.
  2. Press Make a game key and name it, for example My obby. Owners and admins only, up to 5 keys per list.
  3. Press Insert. The key goes straight into StartReports, so you never have to copy it by hand.
  4. Insert adds a CheckpointLTC folder to ServerScriptService with two scripts, CheckpointLTCReports and StartReports, with the key filled into StartReports. Studio asks once if CheckpointLTC can add scripts. Press Allow, then Insert again.
  5. In Game Settings, Security, turn on Allow HTTP Requests and publish.
  6. Best: move the key into your game's Secrets. In the Creator Dashboard open your game, then Secrets, Create Secret. Name it CheckpointLTC, paste the key, set the domain to checkpointltc.ltclevtec.com, then clear the key out of StartReports. For Studio playtests add the same secret under File, Experience Settings, Security, Local Secrets.
  7. Call the CheckpointLTCReport RemoteFunction from your own report button.

Safe by default

  • Filtered by Roblox on your server before it's sent. The server refuses anything that wasn't filtered.
  • Up to 500 characters. One report per player per hour across every server, 120 per game per hour.
  • Flood protection. If one player floods reports, the key is treated as stolen and turned off. You get a notification, then make a new key and update your secret.
  • The key stays on the server, in Secrets or StartReports, where players can't see it. Revoke a key any time.

Player reports: the inbox

  • Each report shows the text, Bug or Feedback, the player's name and when.
  • Approve turns it into a task in an Inbox section, labelled Bug for bug reports, with a note saying who reported it.
  • Dismiss clears it without making a task.
  • Testing in Studio works too. Reports from an unpublished place or a playtest come through the same way.

Player reports: when a key gets turned off

Your game only lets each player send one report an hour. If one player sends far more than that, someone outside your game has the key and is calling CheckpointLTC with it directly. The key is turned off on the spot.

What happens

  • The key stops working straight away for every server of your game. Players see "Reports are off right now."
  • The list owner and every admin get a red alert in Needs fixing. The toolbar dot lights up.
  • The report that got through before the cutoff is still in the inbox, so you can see what they sent.

What to do

  1. Press Fix it. It opens In game reports for that list.
  2. Press Make a game key and Insert, or put the new key in your game's Secrets.
  3. Delete the turned off key.

The alert clears from your count when you press Fix it. After that it can be deleted like any other notification.

Teams and tidying up

Share lists, pick roles, keep track of your own work, and clean up safely.

People and sharing

  • Share code. Anyone with the 8 letter code can open the list and view it. Codes skip letters that look alike, like 0 and O, or 1, I and L.
  • New code stops the old one working and removes everyone who joined with it, right away. People added by name keep access.
  • Add a person by Roblox username or UserId. They join as your default role, editor unless you changed it in Settings. Owners also get Add an admin.
  • Assign the list puts it under that person's Assigned to me, view only. That's different from assigning a single task.
  • Role and Remove appear on each person, so you can change someone later. Admins can't change other admins.

Read, delete and undo

  • Open on a card takes you to the task or list and marks it read.
  • Tick boxes pick cards. Select all and Unselect all sit at the top.
  • Mark read clears the ticked ones. With nothing ticked it says Mark all read.
  • Delete moves the ticked ones to the Trash. Press Undo on the toast, or restore them from Trash later.
  • Read cards stay on the page, greyed out and below the unread ones, until you delete them.
  • 30 days. Notifications clear on their own 30 days after they were sent, and you keep your newest 100.
  • Only you see your notifications. Marking one read or deleting it never touches anyone else's copy.

Due dates, fixes and updates

Due dates

  • Shows up when a task given to you is overdue or due within a day.
  • Open task jumps to it and marks it read. Marking it done makes it go away.

Needs fixing

  • No tick box until you press Fix it. These can't be cleared without fixing them.

Updates

  • Says which version is out and how to update: Plugins, Manage Plugins, Update. It should be good to go, if not reopen Studio.
  • Got it marks it read until the next version.

The red dot

  • On the toolbar icon whenever any tab has something waiting.

Trash

  • 30 days. Deleted lists, tasks and notifications wait here, then they're erased for good. Notifications sit under their own heading.
  • Restore on any row brings one back.
  • Tap rows to tick them, or Select all, then Restore or Delete the ticked ones in one go.
  • Empty trash erases everything you're allowed to erase. It asks first.
  • Deleting a list moves it and all its tasks here, and everyone you shared it with loses access straight away.

Activity log

List menu, Activity log. Every change, who made it and when, newest first. Owners see all of it, editors see the latest 50. It keeps up to 500 events for 90 days.

It records lists being made, imported, renamed, archived and pinned, new share codes, tasks being added, edited, moved, checked off, deleted, restored and commented on, people being added, removed or given a new role, list assignments, approved player reports and undos.

Export

List menu, Export as Markdown or Export as JSON. Copy the link into a browser to download. It works for 10 minutes. Due dates come out in your own time with your UTC offset at the end, so 23:59 UTC-05:00 is 11:59 PM Eastern. Here's the Markdown export of the example list:

Support

Report a bug

  • Title, up to 100 characters, and what happened, up to 2000.
  • Steps to reproduce.
  • Plugin version and Studio info are included by default.
  • One per hour.

Send feedback and attach a file

  • Title, your idea, and an optional category: UI, Feature or Other. One per hour.
  • Tick I understand once, and it's remembered for your account.
  • One file up to 10 MB: png, jpg, mp4, mov or txt.
Keep it clean. Every file is checked automatically before anyone sees it. Inappropriate uploads mean a strike and a permanent upload ban. Severe violations mean a ban from CheckpointLTC, and illegal content is reported to the authorities.

Devices and logging out

  • Every computer you're logged in on is listed with when it was last used. The one you're on says "this one".
  • Sign out on any row logs that computer out.
  • Up to 10 devices. Logging in on an eleventh signs out the oldest.
  • Log out on this computer sits at the bottom of every Settings tab. Your lists stay on the server.
  • Lost a computer? Sign it out here, or remove CheckpointLTC from your Roblox account's connected apps.

Privacy and getting help

What CheckpointLTC knows about you

  • Your Roblox UserId, so lists know who owns them.
  • Whether you own the plugin, for paid versions.
  • Your lists and what's in them.

No email, no password, and nothing from your places. Studio links store a path and a marker id, not your scripts. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Only install the official copy

Get CheckpointLTC from its Creator Store page or the link in my Discord. Reuploaded plugins on the Toolbox sometimes have backdoors added. The official one only talks to the CheckpointLTC server and Roblox.
