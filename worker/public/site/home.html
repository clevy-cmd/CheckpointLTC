Right where you build.

The window floats over the 3D view next to your Explorer. Drag it, resize it, close it. It opens right where you left it, and a badge on the toolbar button tells you when something changed while it was closed.

My lists

Everything about a task, one click away.

Click a task and its details slide in from the side. Every change saves on its own, and your teammates see it on their next refresh.

  • Priority, due dates and labels for Scripting, UI, VFX and Bug
  • Sections and notes so a big list stays readable
  • Assign it to one person and it shows up in their My tasks
  • Comments with @mentions that ping the right person
  • Who changed it last, and when, right under the title

List or board. Or just press /.

Flip any list into a board with a column per section, then drag tasks across. Sort by your own order, priority, due date or what changed last.

The command bar finds lists, tasks and actions from a few letters. Press / with your mouse over the window, type, and hit Enter.

Starting fresh? Make a blank list, or start from the Game launch checklist or Bug triage templates.

Your TODOs, turned into tasks.

Write a TODO comment where the work is. CheckpointLTC finds it and keeps a link back to that exact line.

  1. Write it. -- TODO: add a stamina bar on line 13 of your SprintBoost script.
  2. Scan. Pick Scan scripts for TODO comments, choose a reminder, add the ones you want.
  3. Jump back. Press Go to SprintBoost line 13 and the editor opens with your cursor on it.

Built for Studio, not bolted on.

  • Link a task to a part, model or script. Click it later and Studio selects it, or opens the script at the right line.
  • Links survive renames and moves, thanks to a tiny hidden marker on the object.
  • Pin a list to your game so it sits at the top whenever anyone opens that place.
  • See who's around in Team Create, and which task they have open right now.
  • Bind your own shortcuts for six actions in Customize Shortcuts.

Bug reports, straight from your players.

Let players send bugs and feedback from inside your live game. Every report is run through Roblox's text filter and lands in your list's inbox, waiting for you.

  1. Make a game key from the list's In game reports inbox.
  2. Press Insert. The scripts go into ServerScriptService with the key already in them.
  3. Hook up your button to the CheckpointLTCReport RemoteFunction.
  4. Approve a report and it becomes a task. Dismiss the rest.

Made for teams.

Press Add a person and type a Roblox username or UserId. Or hand out an 8 letter share code, and make a new one whenever you want to cut off old access.

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerEverything, including archive and delete
AdminManage people, rename, pin, game keys
EditorAdd, edit, move and check off tasks
ViewerSee the list, check off their own tasks
AssignedSee it under Assigned to me

My tasks gathers everything assigned to you across every list. Search looks through every list you can see. The bell catches mentions and new tasks, and a reminder pops up when something of yours is due within the hour.

Nothing gets lost.

Deleted something by accident? It sits in the trash for 30 days, and undo takes back your own last change, one at a time. The activity log shows who changed what, and when.

Export any list as Markdown or JSON, with due dates in your own time and your UTC offset. Import one back, or write a Markdown file by hand.
