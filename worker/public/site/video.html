See it in action.

Logging in with Roblox from Studio and making your first list, in about 30 seconds.

Heads up This video shows an older version of the plugin. A new one that matches the updated look is coming soon. The login steps are still the same.

What happens in the video

Tap a time to jump straight to that moment.

  1. In Studio, click Log in with Roblox. The plugin shows a link and a code.
  2. Open the link. The CheckpointLTC page shows the same code, so you know it's your login.
  3. Roblox asks for permission: read your User ID, and read your inventory to check you own the plugin.
  4. Approve it. Roblox sends you back and the page says you're logged in.
  5. Back in Studio, the plugin has logged in on its own.
  6. Make a list, add a task, and choose who can see and edit it.
