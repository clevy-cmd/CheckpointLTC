1.1.11.1.01.0.0

1.1.1 Latest

November 28, 2026

  • More than one person per task. Give a task to up to 10 people. Their avatars show right on the task.
  • Auto assign. Owners and admins can pick people that every new task on a list goes to. You can still change it on each task.
  • Split a task. Turn one long task into several. They stay in the same section and keep the priority, label, due date and people. Undo puts it back.
  • The whole task text. The task panel shows it in full, with a Collapse text button. Notes grow down instead of covering the other fields.
  • Fuller rows. Tasks in the list fill their row with up to 5 lines of text, with a thin divider between tasks.
  • Send and save buttons next to every typing box, plus a Task updated or Notes saved message so you know it went through.
  • Fixes. Banners sit above the typing box. Floating panels don't let clicks or hovers through to things behind them anymore. Deleting a comment updates right away, and so do the task counts in the header and sidebar.

1.1.0

November 27, 2026

  • A real Notifications page with tabs for Needs fixing, Due dates, Tasks, Shared and Updates.
  • A red dot on the toolbar icon when something needs you, even with the window closed.
  • Tick to clean up. Tick notifications to mark them read or delete them. Deleted ones go to the Trash, and you can restore them.
  • Due dates in one place. Due and overdue tasks show in Notifications instead of popping up.
  • Safer game keys. Keep yours in your game's Secrets. If a key gets flooded with reports, it gets turned off for you.
  • One report an hour. Players can send one report an hour, across every server.
  • Long text that doesn't fit ends in ..., and you can hover to read it all.

1.0.0 The original

November 25, 2026

The first public release. Everything the plugin started with:

  • Shared lists with share codes, roles for owners, admins, editors and viewers, and whole lists assigned to people. You log in with Roblox.
  • Tasks with everything on them. Sections, priorities, labels, due dates, notes and comments with @mentions.
  • Studio links to parts, models and script lines, and a TODO scan that turns your comments into tasks.
  • Board view, search across every list, and the / command bar.
  • In game reports from your players, straight into the list's inbox.
  • Import and export, trash and undo, an activity log, and a Download Guide button in the plugin.
